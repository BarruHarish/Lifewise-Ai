# LifeWise AI — Hackathon & Demo Pitch Guide for Judges

## 1. The Elevator Pitch (30-45 Seconds)
> *"Judges, over 90% of Indians either lack life insurance or hold inadequate, low-yield policies sold by aggressive agents pursuing commissions. When a family loses a breadwinner, arbitrary '10x income' rules of thumb fail because they ignore actual inflation, ongoing debts like home loans, and children's education milestones.*
> 
> *We built **LifeWise AI** — an intelligent, transparent life insurance needs finder and digital advisory platform. LifeWise AI replaces agent bias with **actuarial discounted cashflow mathematics (Net Needs)**, compares it against **Human Life Value (HLV)**, provides deterministic product category recommendations, and lets users simulate budget scenarios before booking an unbiased digital consultation with certified advisors."*

---

## 2. Core Problem & Fintech Market Context
| The Industry Problem | How LifeWise AI Solves It |
|---|---|
| **Mis-selling & Agent Bias:** Agents push high-commission Endowment/ULIP plans with poor mortality cover (only 10x premium). | **Unbiased Transparency:** We don't sell policies or take commissions. We explain *why* Term Life is essential for large coverage gaps and show tradeoffs objectively. |
| **Flawed Rules of Thumb:** Traditional portals use simplistic formulas like `10x Annual Income`, ignoring liabilities or inflation. | **Actuarial Net Needs Engine:** Incorporates 6% inflation, 7.5% corpus investment yield, outstanding debts, and existing liquid assets. |
| **Black Box AI vs. Trust:** Users don't trust unexplained numbers generated by generic LLM prompts. | **Explainable Deterministic Math:** Clean, auditable mathematical logic with published assumptions and no hallucinations. |

## 3. Step-by-Step 3-Minute Live Demo Walkthrough

### Step 1: The Landing Page (`http://localhost:3000`)
* **What to Show:**
  - The hero section: *"Find the Right Life Cover for Your Future."*
  - The **Interactive Coverage Preview** card on the right — drag the income and debt sliders to show live Indian Rupee (`₹25,00,000`) formatting.
* **What to Say:**
  > *"Users land on a clean, Stripe/Linear-inspired fintech interface designed to build immediate confidence. Rather than asking for a phone number upfront for spam marketing, we offer immediate interactive clarity."*

### Step 2: The Assessment Wizard (`/assessment`)
* **What to Show:**
  - Click **"Fill Editable Sample Profile"** (top right) to show a real-world salaried profile (Age 30, ₹15L income, 2 dependents, ₹40L home loan).
  - Walk through the 4 steps: Personal Info ➔ Dependents ➔ Liabilities & Future Goals ➔ Existing Assets & Budget.
* **What to Say:**
  > *"Our 4-step wizard validates and preserves user inputs across sessions. Notice we ask for outstanding loans, education goals, and existing protection assets—guaranteeing we don't under-insure or double-count needs."*

### Step 3: Financial Needs & Mathematical Breakdown (`/results`)
* **What to Show:**
  - The **Estimated Additional Cover Required** card.
  - The **Recharts Donut Chart** showing needs distribution (Living support vs. Home Loan vs. Goals).
  - The **Comparison Bar Chart** (Total Needs vs. Existing Cover vs. Coverage Gap).
  - The **Human Life Value (HLV)** benchmark card below it.
* **What to Say:**
  > *"Here is our core innovation: we display both the **Net Needs Approach** (what the family actually needs to survive and extinguish debt) and the **Human Life Value (HLV)** (the economic present value of future career earnings) side-by-side as a comparative benchmark, without double-counting."*

### Step 4: Explainable Product Category Matching (`/recommendations`)
* **What to Show:**
  - Filter tabs across **Term Life**, **Whole Life**, **Endowment**, and **ULIPs**.
  - Show the **Fit Score** (e.g., 95/100 for Term Life), key benefits, limitations, risk considerations, and **Questions to Ask Insurers**.
* **What to Say:**
  > *"Unlike aggregators that push paid insurer sponsor listings, LifeWise AI evaluates product structures deterministically. We explicitly show why Term Insurance is the primary tool for debt shielding, while pointing out the 4.5%–6% return caps on Endowments and market risks on ULIPs."*

### Step 5: Interactive Scenario Simulator (`/simulator`)
* **What to Show:**
  - Move the **Desired Life Cover** slider and **Monthly Budget** slider.
  - Show the dynamic **Affordability Indicator** turning green (Affordable) or red (Stretched) with exact monthly surplus/deficit figures and category cost comparisons.
* **What to Say:**
  > *"Planning is iterative. Users can simulate tenure up to retirement and see in real time whether their monthly budget covers the required term cover."*

### Step 6: Simulated Digital Advisory & Instant Booking (`/advisory`)
* **What to Show:**
  - Select an advisor (e.g., Priya Sharma, Senior Protection Specialist & CFP®).
  - Pick Video or Phone, select a date and time slot, and submit the booking.
  - Show the **instant booking confirmation modal** and celebrate with confetti!
  - Test the **FAQ Chat Assistant** at the bottom (e.g., type *"What is Claim Settlement Ratio?"* or *"Tell me about Section 80C"*).
* **What to Say:**
  > *"Finally, users can book an unbiased consultation with certified CFP/IRDAI specialists with zero sales pressure, or get instant terminology answers from our reviewed assistant."*

## 4. Technical Architecture Highlights (For Tech Judges)
- **Frontend:** Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Recharts for responsive visualizations.
- **Backend:** FastAPI, Python 3.11, Pydantic V2 schemas for strict input validation.
- **Database:** SQLAlchemy ORM with SQLite local fallback and PostgreSQL/Alembic support.
- **Quality & Reliability:** 12 automated unit and API integration tests in Pytest covering edge cases (zero liabilities, existing coverage exceeding needs, HLV career horizons).
- **Security & Privacy:** Compliant with DPDP guidelines — no mandatory login barrier for calculations, zero sensitive KYC data requested, client-side offline calculation fallback.

## 5. Potential Judge Questions & Winning Answers

**Q1: Why didn't you just use a generative AI prompt (like GPT/Claude) to recommend policies?**
> *"In consumer financial planning, generative AI can hallucinate calculations, invent non-existent interest rates, and produce non-deterministic advice that violates regulatory standards. We separated concerns: we use **deterministic actuarial math** for exact calculations and recommendations, ensuring 100% compliance, auditability, and trust."*

**Q2: What is the monetization model if you don't take insurer commissions?**
> *"We operate on a transparent digital advisory model: a flat-fee subscription or fixed consultation fee for unbiased digital advisory sessions, and B2B white-label SaaS for employee financial wellness platforms and wealth managers."*

**Q3: How do you handle inflation and discounting?**
> *"We use an inflation rate of 6% and a safe protection corpus reinvestment yield of 7.5%, resulting in a net real discount rate of ~1.4%. The present value of future recurring family expenses is calculated using an inflation-adjusted annuity discounting formula."*
